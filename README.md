## 🧑‍💻 About Me


- 🎓 **Education:** AI Engineering Student @ **EPITA**
- 🧠 **Interests:** NLP, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, & Reinforcement Learning
- ⚡ **Fun Facts:** I play **Piano** 🎹 and hit the **Gym** 🏋️


I'm passionate about **Computer Vision**, **Reinforcement Learning** and specialized in **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**. Beyond just using APIs, I possess a deep understanding of **Deep Learning Architectures**, with a focus on implementing and dissecting **Transformers**, Attention Mechanisms, CNNs and many others. I thrive on research-oriented challenges, such as building a Deep Q-Network from scratch (see my **RL_DQN** repo!). I also have experience **fine-tuning Large Language Models** for specific domains. On the **MLOps** side, I ensure reproducibility using model versioning (**MLflow**) and manage infrastructure as code with **Terraform**.
